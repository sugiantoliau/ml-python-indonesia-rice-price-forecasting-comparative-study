# A Comparative Study of Time Series Forecasting for Rice Prices Based on Quality Classification in Indonesia / Studi Komparatif Time Series Forecasting Harga Beras Berdasarkan Klasifikasi Kualitas di Indonesia

A Python-based machine learning project that builds a Decision Support System to forecast national monthly rice prices in Indonesia across three quality classifications (Premium, Medium, and SPHP) using data from January 2021 to February 2026. By utilizing Time Series Forecasting models like Linear Regression and Random Forest Regressor, this study provides stakeholders with accurate predictive tools to mitigate inflation risks, protect consumer purchasing power, and implement preventive measures to maintain national rice price stability.

## 🚀 Project Overview & Objectives

The primary objective of this research is to develop an accurate, machine learning-driven predictive tool that serves as a **Decision Support System (DSS)** for key stakeholders in Indonesia. By forecasting national rice price fluctuations, this system aims to help authorities and policymakers take preventive actions to:
* **Mitigate Inflation Risks:** Anticipate sudden spikes in food inflation driven by rice price volatility.
* **Protect Consumer Purchasing Power:** Ensure rice remains affordable for all segments of society.
* **Maintain Price Stability:** Support national food security strategies through data-backed insights.

## 📊 Scope & Dataset

* **Data Source:** Public monthly national consumer-level rice price dataset retrieved from **data.badanpangan.go.id**.
* **Timeframe:** January 2021 to February 2026.
* **Target Commodities:** The study specifically analyzes and compares three major categories of rice in Indonesia:
  1. **Beras Premium** (Premium Rice)
  2. **Beras Medium** (Medium Rice)
  3. **Beras SPHP** (Stabilisasi Pasokan dan Harga Pangan - Government Price Stabilization Rice)

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Algorithms

* **Language:** Python 3.11+
* **Core Libraries:** 
  * `Pandas` & `NumPy` (Data Manipulation & Preprocessing)
  * `Scikit-learn` (Model Training & Evaluation)
  * `Matplotlib` & `Seaborn` (Data Visualization & Trend Analysis)
* **Machine Learning Models Evaluated:**
  * **Linear Regression** (Baseline statistical forecasting)
  * **Random Forest Regressor** (Ensemble-based non-linear forecasting)
